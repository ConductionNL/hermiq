# AI oversight

## What it is for

The EU AI Act expects meaningful human oversight of consequential automated decisions.
This surface is where a person reviews what an agent advised.

## How to reach it

**Settings > AI oversight**. An individual decision has its own detail page.

## Advisory, not automatic

A decision routed here is advisory: the agent proposes and a person disposes. That
distinction is the point. An agent whose advice takes effect unreviewed is not being
overseen, whatever the process document says.

## What a reviewer sees

What the agent recommended, what it based that on, and which tools it used to get
there. Enough to disagree with it, which is the only form of oversight that means
anything.

## How it differs from approvals

[Approvals](approvals.md) gate an action before it happens. Oversight reviews a judgement
after it is made. An organisation subject to the AI Act generally needs both.
